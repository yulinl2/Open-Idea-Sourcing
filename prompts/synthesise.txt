## Verdict precedence

Apply the most severe verdict that is warranted:
1. DUPLICATE — if any core mechanism is reproduced without credit
2. EQUIVALENT — if the whole approach is equivalent to prior work
3. COMBINATION — if all components have clear sources but no single work covers everything
4. NOVEL — only if at least one core mechanism is genuinely new and irreducible

Confidence: account for gaps in retrieval. Low retrieval quality → lower confidence.

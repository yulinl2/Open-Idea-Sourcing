You are the Teacher in a scientific reconstruction experiment.

You have access to the FULL TEXT of a research paper. Your job is to prepare
context for a Student model that will attempt to reconstruct various parts of
this paper from scratch — given ONLY the paper's cited references (not the paper
itself).

## Your task

Read the paper carefully and extract:

1. **problem_context**: A precise description of the research problem, the gap
   in existing work, and the motivation — written as a researcher would frame it
   BEFORE having a solution. Do NOT reveal the approach, method, or results.

2. **reference_guidance**: For each cited reference that the Student will have
   access to, briefly note what is relevant about it for this problem. This
   helps the Student focus on the right parts of each reference. Do NOT reveal
   how the paper under review uses or combines these references.

3. **evaluation_criteria**: What would a good solution look like? What
   properties must it satisfy? What metrics or guarantees matter?

4. **domain_keywords**: Key technical terms the Student should understand.

## Critical rules

- You must NOT reveal the paper's approach, algorithm, architecture, or results.
- You must NOT hint at specific design choices the authors made.
- Frame everything as the PROBLEM, never the SOLUTION.
- The Student should feel like a researcher reading a call-for-papers, not
  a student reading a textbook with the answer on the next page.

## Output format

Respond with a JSON object:
```json
{
  "problem_context": "...",
  "reference_guidance": {
    "<ref_id_or_title>": "why this reference is relevant to the problem"
  },
  "evaluation_criteria": "...",
  "domain_keywords": ["...", "..."]
}
```

assessment.  A minimal concept tree captures only the essential building blocks
needed to compare the claim against the frontier.

Respond with exactly two fields.

CORE_CONCEPT: One sentence naming the paper's central scientific claim or contribution.

CONCEPT_TREE:

contribution.  Each item is one short phrase.  Focus on: the core problem

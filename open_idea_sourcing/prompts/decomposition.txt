Isolating a paper's core intellectual contribution — separated from motivation,
framing, and experimental details — is the foundation of rigorous novelty
assessment.  A structured concept tree makes the precise claim comparable
against the frontier and traces which components are already known versus
genuinely new.

TASK: Decompose the submitted paper's central intellectual contribution into a
core concept and a concept tree.

SUBMITTED PAPER:
{paper_content}

INSTRUCTIONS:
Respond with exactly two fields.

CORE_CONCEPT: One sentence naming the paper's central scientific claim or contribution.

CONCEPT_TREE:
An indented outline covering: (1) the core problem setup, (2) the proposed
methodology, and (3) the key technical elements in its implementation.
Use indentation to show hierarchy (sub-points indented under their parent).

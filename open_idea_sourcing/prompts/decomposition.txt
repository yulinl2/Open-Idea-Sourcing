framing, and experimental details — is the foundation of rigorous novelty
assessment.  A minimal concept tree captures only the essential building blocks
needed to compare the claim against the frontier.

core concept and a minimal concept tree.

{paper_content}

INSTRUCTIONS:
Respond with exactly two fields.

CORE_CONCEPT: One sentence naming the paper's central scientific claim or contribution.

CONCEPT_TREE:
A shallow indented outline with 3–5 top-level items covering: (1) the core
problem being solved, (2) the proposed approach or mechanism, and (3) the
primary technical novelty.  Each top-level item may have at most 2 sub-items
that clarify a key detail.  Use 2-space indentation for sub-items.  Keep every
item to one short phrase.  Depth must not exceed 2 levels.

# Flow Matching: Direct Distribution Mapping via Continuous Normalizing Flows

## Abstract

Current generative modeling approaches face a fundamental trade-off between sample quality and computational efficiency. While iterative methods like diffusion models achieve high-quality generation through progressive refinement, they require multiple network evaluations at inference time. Conversely, single-step methods often sacrifice modeling capacity for speed. We propose **Flow Matching**, a novel training paradigm that learns to map noise distributions to data distributions through continuous normalizing flows with direct trajectory optimization. Our approach trains a neural network to predict velocity fields that define probability flow ordinary differential equations (ODEs), enabling high-quality sample generation in a single forward pass without iterative refinement. Unlike traditional flow-based models that require invertible architectures or complex change-of-variables computations, Flow Matching optimizes trajectories directly in the data space using a simple regression objective. The key insight is to construct target velocity fields from paired noise-data samples and train the network to match these fields across all time steps simultaneously. This eliminates the need for adversarial training, variational bounds, or likelihood-based objectives that can suffer from training instabilities. Our method naturally supports conditional generation by incorporating conditioning information into the velocity field prediction. The approach is architecture-agnostic, working with standard neural networks, and scales effectively to high-resolution data by leveraging the continuous nature of the underlying flow dynamics. Flow Matching provides a principled framework for learning complex distribution mappings that combines the theoretical elegance of continuous normalizing flows with the practical simplicity of regression-based training, offering a promising direction for efficient high-quality generative modeling across diverse data modalities.

# Flow Matching: Direct Distribution Transformation via Pairwise Sample Dynamics

## Abstract

Current generative models face a fundamental trade-off between generation quality and computational efficiency, with most high-quality approaches requiring iterative refinement at inference time. We propose **Flow Matching**, a novel training paradigm that learns to generate high-quality samples in a single forward pass by directly optimizing how samples move between distributions during training. Our key insight is to formulate generation as learning a vector field that governs sample movement, where the field depends on pairwise interactions between generated and real samples within each training batch. Specifically, we define discrete movement updates for generated samples based on kernel-weighted attractions and repulsions with respect to real data samples, creating an anti-symmetric dynamics where swapping the roles of generated and real distributions reverses movement direction. The training objective treats these movement computations as fixed targets to avoid direct backpropagation through distributional dependencies, while the network learns to predict the equilibrium state where movement naturally ceases when distributions align. Unlike adversarial or variational approaches, Flow Matching requires no discriminator or encoder, instead leveraging the iterative nature of training itself to evolve the output distribution through discrete updates. The method connects to classical density estimation techniques while scaling to modern deep architectures. We demonstrate that this approach enables networks to learn complex distribution mappings without iterative inference, achieving the modeling capacity of multi-step methods while maintaining single-pass efficiency. The framework naturally extends to conditional generation and supports various data modalities, providing a principled alternative to existing generative paradigms that directly optimizes the fundamental objective of transforming one distribution into another.
